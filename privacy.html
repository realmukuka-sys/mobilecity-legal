MOBILECITY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MobileCity ("we", "us", "the app") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our mobile application available on Google Play and the App Store.

1. What we collect

We collect the name, phone number, email address, and delivery address you give us when you create an account or place an order. We also collect anonymous app usage data (e.g. which categories are popular) to help us improve the product.

2. Search history

When you use the search bar we record your queries locally on your device so the "Recommended for you" section can show items you may like. This history never leaves the device unless you explicitly enable cloud sync.

3. How we use your information

  • To fulfil your orders and arrange delivery.
  • To send dispatch notifications, promotions and customer-support replies.
  • To improve the app's features, performance and reliability.
  • To prevent fraud and keep your account secure.

We do not sell your data to advertisers.

4. Payment data

Card details are processed by our payment partners (Visa, Mastercard) and are never stored on our servers. Mobile Money confirmations contain only a transaction reference. We do not see or retain your card PAN, expiry date or CVV.

5. Photos and camera

If you choose to add a profile photo or upload an image to your listing, we access your device's photo library or camera with your permission. The selected image is stored on your device and only uploaded to our servers when you publish content.

6. Notifications

We send push notifications for order updates and occasional marketing announcements. You can turn marketing notifications off at any time from your device settings.

7. Sharing your data

We share the minimum information required with our third-party service providers — payment gateways, delivery partners, and cloud infrastructure — strictly for the purpose of operating the service. Each provider is bound by confidentiality and data-protection obligations.

8. Your rights

  • You can edit your profile from the Profile screen at any time.
  • You can request a copy of your data by emailing us.
  • You can delete your account from the Profile screen. Deletion removes all personally-identifying records within 30 days.

9. Children

MobileCity is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page tells you when it last changed. Continued use of the app after changes are posted means you accept the new policy.

11. Contact us

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email privacy@mobilecity.zm or write to us at MobileCity, Lusaka, Zambia.
