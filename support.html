Common questions

How do I track my order?

Open the Orders tab in the app. Your status moves through Waiting for dispatch → Dispatched → Delivered, and you'll get an in-app alert each time it changes.

What payment methods do you accept?

Mobile Money (Airtel, MTN, Zamtel) and Visa / Mastercard debit and credit cards. Pick at checkout.

How fast is delivery?

Same-day for Lusaka, 1–3 working days for other towns. You'll see the estimate at checkout once you choose your store.

Can I return an item?

Yes — faulty items can be returned within 7 days for inspection. Original packaging and accessories must be included. Email support@mobilecity.zm with your order ID to start a return.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile → Delete account. Type "DELETE" to confirm. Personal data is removed within 30 days.

I forgot my password.

Tap "Sign in" on the Profile tab, then "Forgot password?" — we'll email you a reset link. If you don't see it, check spam or write to us directly.

Bug or feature request?

Inside the app: Profile → Help us improve. You can tag your message as feedback, bug report, feature request, or order help, and our team triages every submission.
